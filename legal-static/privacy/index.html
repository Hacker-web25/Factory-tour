1.Who we are and what this covers

VPV (Virtual Plant Visit) is a 360° virtual-tour platform operated by The Next Design. We help manufacturers host immersive tours of their facilities and share them with their own customers through private links.

This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It applies to the VPV website, dashboards, tour viewer and presenter tools. We handle personal data in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, to the extent each applies and is in force.

Two roles, in plain words
For data about your own team and your billing (accounts, payments, security logs), VPV decides how the data is used. For data about your Viewers and your meetings (tour analytics, client details, presentation recordings), you decide why it is collected and VPV processes it on your behalf. Your organisation is responsible for having a lawful basis and any notice or consent that its own law requires for that second category.

3.What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or marketing networks.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking, and we do not set tracking cookies.
  • We do not fingerprint people across websites. The device identifier is a random ID kept in your own browser and scoped to our domain.
  • We do not use your content, recordings or transcripts to train AI models, and we send an AI provider only the transcript text needed to return a result.
  • VPV does not join your calls or record Viewers. Recording happens only on a presenter’s own device when the presenter switches it on.

4.Recordings, location and AI analysis

These features are optional and controlled by the presenter and the organisation.

  • Recording. The recorder captures the presenter’s microphone. If the presenter also chooses to share a browser tab or system audio, the recording can include the sound of that tab — which may be other participants of a video call. The browser asks for permission each time. Audio is stored in your organisation’s private storage area and is visible only to people your organisation authorises.
  • Transcription. Speech is turned into text in the presenter’s browser, using the browser’s built-in speech recognition and, where available, an on-device model. Built-in recognition is provided by the browser maker (for example Google in Chrome, or Microsoft in Edge) and may process audio on its servers under that company’s own terms. VPV does not send the audio file to an AI provider.
  • AI analysis. If an organisation asks for it, the transcript text (not the audio) is sent to an AI provider — NVIDIA’s hosted inference service or Google’s Gemini API — to separate speakers and produce a summary and score. If neither is available, a rule-based method runs inside VPV instead.
  • Location. If the presenter allows it in the browser prompt, we store the coordinates and look up a readable place name from them. Declining the prompt does not affect the presentation.

Consent. The organisation must inform everyone on a recorded conversation and obtain the consent applicable law requires before recording. Under our Terms this is the organisation’s responsibility, and it can delete any recording at any time.

5.How we use personal data

  • To provide, secure and support the Service you have bought.
  • To take payments, issue GST invoices and meet tax and accounting duties.
  • To send service messages: sign-in and new-device alerts, password resets, invoices, renewal reminders, support replies, and notices of changes to prices, terms or this policy. These are not marketing; you cannot opt out of them while you have an account, because they carry information you need.
  • To detect abuse and protect other customers.
  • To fix defects and improve the Service, using aggregated or de-identified data where possible.
  • To comply with law and respond to lawful requests.

7.Where data is stored and transferred

Your data is stored mainly in Frankfurt, Germany, and processed on global infrastructure operated by the providers above. Because VPV is operated from India, this involves transferring data across borders. We use providers that apply encryption in transit and access controls, and we transfer personal data only to countries and providers permitted under Indian law.

8.Cookies and local storage

VPV does not set tracking cookies. It uses a small amount of browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember settings. The details are in our Cookie Policy.

9.How we protect data

Every database table is protected by row-level security so one organisation cannot read another’s data. Traffic is encrypted, panorama files use signed URLs, sign-in is rate-limited, and active devices are capped. Read the full list on the Security page. No online service can promise perfect security; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

11.Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data and a summary of how it is used;
  • correct or update data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete data we no longer need to keep;
  • stop processing that relies on your consent, by withdrawing it; and
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you are unable to.

Write to privacy@myvpv.com. We may need to verify who you are, and we reply within 30 days. For tour content and Viewer analytics, the request should come from your organisation’s administrator, because the organisation controls that data. If a Viewer contacts us about a tour, we will pass the request to the organisation that shared it.

12.Children

VPV is a business product and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we hold data about a child, write to us and we will delete it.

13.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. For material changes we will e-mail the administrator on record and show a notice on the sign-in page at least 14 days before they take effect. The “Effective” date at the top always shows the current version. Changes to prices are covered separately in the Terms: we give 30 days’ prior notice by e-mail.

14.Grievance officer and contact

If you have a concern about how your data is handled, contact our grievance officer. We acknowledge complaints within two business days and aim to resolve them within 30 days.
