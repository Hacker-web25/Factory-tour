# VPV pricing model — my recommendation

**Model:** 4 core tiers + à-la-carte add-ons. Anchor high, make **Team** the obvious buy, use **Solo Pro** as a decoy that pushes serious users to Team.

Illustrative INR (annual billing shown as monthly). Numbers are hypotheses to test — the *structure* matters more than the exact digits.

---

## Tier 1 — Solo Starter (funnel)

**Who** — one person testing / hobby / MSME testing the waters
**Price** — **Free** *or* ₹999/mo (decide based on whether you need funnel volume vs. instant revenue)
**Watermark** — **Powered by VPV** stays on
**Includes**
- 1 tour, up to 10 scenes, 2 GB storage, 500 views/mo
- Every "Starter" and "Core" feature in the catalogue
- Public URL sharing, embed, one language
- Basic transitions (fade/zoom/slide/instant), auto-tour, glass viewer UI

**Purpose** — funnel + growth. Every shared tour advertises you. Watermark + view cap are the pressure points that push upgrade.

---

## Tier 2 — Solo Pro (decoy + serious solo)

**Who** — one serious solo seller / consultant / small studio
**Price** — **₹2,999–3,999/mo**
**Includes** — everything in Starter, plus:
- 10 tours, 25 scenes/tour, 20 GB storage, 5k views/mo
- **Remove watermark**
- Full hotspot library (video, audio, PDF, custom icons)
- **Photoshop-style Edit tab** (colour grading + presets)
- **Warp** cinematic transition + camera momentum
- Micro-interactions (breathing / hover / ripple)
- Sticky style, copy-paste style
- Password-protected unlisted tours
- Asset library

**Purpose (decoy math)** — Solo Pro is priced deliberately close to Team-entry so a 2-3-person shop reasons *"for a little more I get the whole team version"* → they self-select up to Team. Solo Pro on its own is still a legitimate product for solo sellers.

---

## Tier 3 — Team ⭐ (target — profit centre)

**Who** — sales team up to 100 (factory / manufacturer / MSME with a real sales function)
**Price** — sold in **seat bands** (avoids sticker shock)

| Band | Seats | Price/mo |
|---|---|---|
| Team 10 | up to 10 | **₹9,999** |
| Team 25 | up to 25 | **₹19,999** |
| Team 50 | up to 50 | **₹39,999** |
| Team 100 | up to 100 | **₹74,999** |

**Highlight this as "Most popular"** on the pricing page.
**Includes** — everything in Solo Pro, plus:
- Unlimited tours, unlimited scenes, 100 GB storage
- **Multi-user org** (org_admin + presenters, role-based access)
- Invite by code / email, per-presenter tour assignment
- **Precise sales analytics** — sessions, day-by-day, per-session start-time + duration
- Scene-by-scene time, hotspot clicks + hovers (named + counted)
- **Real-time dashboards** + working notifications bell
- **GPS location per presentation** + map link
- **Auto voice recording** + live transcript + AI topic detection (free browser stack)
- Team insights (top presenter / idle / momentum / countries)
- Team calendar + meeting reminders
- Warp + Blur, Dissolve cinematics, idle showcase spin
- Master hotspots, magnetic snap + guides, select-all + bulk edit
- Polygon hotspots, 3D overlay modes
- Offline mode (pre-download for site visits)
- Menu builder wizard
- Presenter share links with attribution
- Priority email support

**Purpose** — this is your money tier. Everything a factory needs to actually **run sales on VPV** lives here.

## Tier 4 — Enterprise (anchor + whales)

**Who** — large org, multi-branch, procurement-driven buyer
**Price** — **"Contact sales" — never show a number** (job of the anchor is to make Team look cheap and to catch the whales)
**Floor** — ₹1.5–3L/yr

**Includes** — everything in Team, plus:
- Unlimited seats
- **Full white-label** (custom logo + colours), custom domain
- Real-time collaborative editing + editor presence
- SSO (Google / Microsoft / SAML), audit log, data residency
- SLA with uptime + response guarantees
- Dedicated success manager
- Custom feature development
- Backup / export / restore
- Advanced AI (diarization + LLM insights via paid engine — see Add-ons)
- Future: dollhouse / mini-map, VR headset mode, guided cinematic intros

**Purpose** — anchors the ceiling, converts a small % of leads at 5-10× Team price. Never publish the price.

## Add-ons (ARPU boosters — sold on top of any paid tier)

These are how you extract meaningfully more from every paid customer.

| Add-on | Sell to | Suggested pricing |
|---|---|---|
| **Custom domain** | Solo Pro+ | ₹1,999/mo or ₹19,999/yr |
| **Extra languages** (AI translate + language pack) | Solo Pro+ | ₹999/mo per language |
| **Extra storage** (100 GB pack) | Team+ | ₹1,499/mo per 100 GB |
| **Extra bandwidth / viewer capacity** | Team+ | ₹1,999/mo per pack |
| **Paid AI insights** (speaker diarization + LLM analysis) | Team+ | ₹2,499/mo per org |
| **Priority "8-hour" support upgrade** | Solo Pro+ | ₹1,999/mo |
| **Onboarding / setup service** *(high-margin one-time)* | any | ₹19,999–49,999 one-time |
| **Done-for-you tour production** *(services)* | any | ₹25,000+ per tour |
| **Live training session** | Team+ | ₹9,999 per session |

Services (onboarding, DIY production, training) are especially valuable in the MSME market — buyers actively want hand-holding and it's high-margin cash.

---

## Levers that quietly boost ARPU

1. **Annual billing default** — show monthly as more expensive. Locks cash + reduces churn.
2. **14-day Team trial** — let paying Solo users taste team analytics/recording; the downgrade-shock pushes them to buy.
3. **Seat bands (not per-seat)** — reduces sticker shock in the top-of-funnel; per-seat pricing surfaces only during negotiation.
4. **Meter enforcement with grace** — soft-cap at 110% of the tier's view/storage limit, then upsell. Never break the buyer's meeting.
5. **Watermark is your growth engine** — every free tour shared advertises VPV. Keep it aggressive.
